How it works

Three steps. Your wallet stays yours.

1

Say what you plan to do

Example: “I want to buy ETH in 15 minutes.” DelayGuard maps that to ETH Up.

2

Approve the protection

DelayGuard finds a live DreamDEX Event Contract. Your wallet approves the real testnet transaction.

3

Keep the proof

The receipt stores the market and Somnia transaction hash. If your side wins, claim it from the same page.

Onchain proof

DelayGuard does not call a local browser record “protected.” The onchain receipt appears only after DreamDEX reports that the order filled.
